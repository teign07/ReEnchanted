# Borrowed Lanterns: the contrary shelf

Added 2026-11-06 to `QuoteLibraryRegistry` in `Shared/ReferenceLibrary.swift`.
The original 67 entries are preserved. These 24 additions share the existing
free pack, four-hour selection cadence, and individual-content rest identity.
They carry the same weight as the existing weight-2 entries.

The additions widen the emotional range: nonsense, disagreement, dry comedy,
appetite, envy, private invention, and the physical strangeness of ordinary
things. They are quotations from other voices, not lines attributed to the Book.
Fictional speakers are identified in the displayed source when relevant.

## Texts checked

Wording was checked against the linked full texts, rather than quotation
aggregators. These are public-domain English works in the editions linked here.
Curly quotation marks/apostrophes and initial capitals are normalized for a
standalone card. Excerpts retain their words; an ending full stop may replace
a dialogue comma. Poetry line breaks are retained. This record covers the new
entries only.
