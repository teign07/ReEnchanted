# ReEnchanted Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-06-19

This is a draft for the public privacy policy page required by App Store
Connect. Review it before publishing, especially if future analytics, sync,
accounts, subscriptions, or server-side AI features are added.

## Summary

ReEnchanted is designed as a local-first private book. The app works with notes,
photos, body signals, weather, location, calendar context, and other personal
material only when you choose to use those features. We do not sell personal
data. We do not use third-party advertising. We do not track you across other
apps or websites.

## Data you may choose to put in the app

You may create or import:

- notes, diary entries, choices, moods, and story fragments
- food, fuel, supplement, medication, rest, and body notes
- photos or generated illuminated artifacts
- custom cast members, places, anchors, and relationship context
- calendar-derived page context if you enable the Calendar Doorway
- HealthKit-derived body signals if you approve HealthKit access
- location/weather context if you tap the Weather or Location features
- portable save files such as `.reenchanted-save.json`

This information is used to create private pages, prompts, local search results,
exports, and optional reminders or calendar events.

## Health and body data

If you approve HealthKit access, ReEnchanted can read selected HealthKit data
such as movement, sleep, heart-related quantities, respiratory quantities, blood
pressure/glucose quantities, body mass, and nutrition quantities. The app uses
these signals to create private Body Pages and supportive reflections.

ReEnchanted is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor,
prescribe, or replace professional medical care. Body and medication-related
text is used only as personal context and may help you prepare a question for a
doctor or pharmacist.

ReEnchanted does not request access to clinical health records.

## Location and weather

When you use weather or location features, the app may use your approximate
current location to fetch local weather or check whether you are near an Anchor
you created. The app uses this context to make pages more relevant. It should
not display raw coordinates in ordinary pages.

## Photos, camera, microphone, and speech

If you choose photos, take photos, or save finished artifacts to Photos, the app
uses those images for the page or artifact you requested. If you tap voice input,
the app uses microphone and speech recognition access to turn speech into text.

## Calendar, reminders, and notifications

If you enable the Calendar Doorway, the app can read nearby calendar events so
the Book can reflect the shape of your day. If you tap an add-to-calendar or
reminder action, the app writes only the event or reminder you requested. If you
enable Book whispers, the app can schedule local notifications.

## App lock

If you enable Protect the Book, ReEnchanted uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your
device passcode through Apple's local authentication system. The app receives
only whether authentication succeeded or failed. It does not receive biometric
data.

## Network use

Some optional features may contact third-party services:

- local weather lookups
- optional food/nutrition lookups when you provide a USDA FoodData Central key
- optional open web or Reddit app-only search paths when configured

These features are used to answer the request you made in the app. Avoid putting
private information into search fields or food lookups unless you are
comfortable sending that query to the relevant service.

## Saving, export, and sharing

Your app data is stored on your device unless you export, share, back up, or
import it through system features such as Files, iCloud backup, Share Sheet, or
Photos. A `.reenchanted-save.json` file can contain sensitive personal material.
Share it only with people or services you trust.

## Children

ReEnchanted is not intended for the Kids Category and is not directed to
children. Do not use the app to collect or share children's personal information
without appropriate consent.
