# Dictionary Rebellion: Lean Content Graph

This is the production board for the first public Dictionary Rebellion in
September 2027. September 2026 is Issue Zero: a TestFlight/Lab rehearsal whose
receipts are development evidence and must not become public participation.
The objective is not to fill every surface with rebellion content. It is to make
one changing condition legible in several places without displacing the
reader's ordinary Book.

## The small version that still feels alive

Immersion comes from **consequence and recurrence**, not raw item count. A few
authored facts should travel sideways: Radio hears the same incident The Bleed
misreports; a class responds to it; a margin contradicts both; a later scene
remembers what the reader ruled. Do not author four unrelated versions merely
to occupy four surfaces.

The minimum lovable month has:

- **4 phase openings** — one unmistakable authored arrival for each state;
- **2 consequential interactive hinges** — returned field evidence and the
  treaty climax;
- **8 marginalia atoms** — two per phase;
- **4 Radio atoms** — one per phase;
- **4 Bleed atoms** — one public account per phase;
- **2 private letters** — one from each pole, Mook and Pippa;
- **1 fieldwork invitation with a return socket**;
- **4 phase-direction packets** — mood and behavior for classes, recipes,
  visuals, and generated connective tissue;
- the substantial negotiation, Lexicon, treaty, Cast, undertaking, and
  physical-edition material already present.

That is **23 small authored atoms plus six dramatic beats**, not hundreds of
Pages. Most atoms are a paragraph or less. The existing systems distribute and
repeat them under cadence control.

## Content graph

```text
SETUP OPENING
  -> first linguistic anomaly
  -> Mook and Pippa take opposing readings
  -> first negotiations become eligible

BUILDUP OPENING
  -> words openly walk out
  -> Academy factions become visible
  -> fieldwork invitation -> returned evidence socket
  -> negotiation rulings accumulate

CLIMAX OPENING
  -> misunderstandings cause a concrete cost
  -> Department announces the Great Recall
  -> earlier rulings + returned evidence enter treaty scene
  -> treaty outcome settles

AFTERMATH OPENING
  -> temporary physics ends
  -> outcome-specific reaction and treaty Page
  -> Lexicon + relationships + selected evidence become relics
  -> one early-October residue may surface; no backlog begins
```

The four openings are historical gates. The two hinges are the only additional
large scenes required for the lean version. Word Negotiations remain the
repeatable participatory middle rather than being inflated into bespoke IF for
every day.

## The six dramatic beats

These are the only substantial pieces to write before September can be read as
one event rather than a collection of exercises.

1. **Setup opening — The Word That Wouldn't Go Back.** Ordinary roll call,
   ordinary Academy, one small refusal. Establish affection for term life before
   breaking it.
2. **Buildup opening — The Walkout.** Words leave in public; Mook calls it a
   breach, Pippa calls it breathing. Make the conflict spatial and visible.
3. **Fieldwork crossing — Evidence From Outside.** Ask for one photographed,
   heard, or copied example of language living beyond its formal definition.
   The submission remains reader evidence.
4. **Climax opening — The Cost of Being Misunderstood.** One concrete Academy
   consequence proves that total semantic freedom is not harmless.
5. **Treaty hinge — The Great Recall.** The Department's forced solution makes
   inaction impossible. The reader's prior rulings and returned evidence enter
   the negotiation; no generic choice screen may substitute for those receipts.
6. **Aftermath opening — The Morning After Meaning.** Show what stopped, what
   changed, and which relic is now physically in this Book before the existing
   outcome Page speaks.

## Production order under time pressure

### Pass 1 — historical spine

Deepen the six scaffolded beat records into the six dramatic beats below and
write the four phase-direction packets. Until this is coherent, do not make
more decorative assets.

### Pass 2 — minimum cross-media proof

Write four Radio pieces, four Bleed pieces, eight marginalia atoms, and Pippa's
private climax letter. Reuse Mook's existing undertaking letter. Each atom must
point to a spine event or consequence; delete any that merely restates the
premise.

### Pass 3 — connect and refine

Keep the stable phase IDs and existing lifecycle route, gate the authored
content correctly, connect the field-evidence receipt to the treaty hinge, and
verify the finished September plus early-October residue in the simulator. Only
then consider extra character scenes, more
marginalia art, bespoke class variants, puzzles, Market wares, or additional
physical leaves.

## Explicitly deferred

- A bespoke scene for every day.
- Unique content for every Academy class in every phase.
- Multiple field missions before the first one proves satisfying.
- More negotiable words; twenty already supply the repeatable middle.
- A different visual system per phase; use one vocabulary with four states.
- More outcome branches before the three existing treaty families read clearly.
- Any medium whose only contribution is “the rebellion is happening here too.”

The acceptance question is not “did every grid cell get filled?” It is:
**Did the reader live through a changing argument about meaning, bring something
real into it, affect its settlement, and find evidence afterward that it had
happened here?**
