# The Unwritten Deck: ReEnchanted revision

The bookcraft principles below remain useful. The reader subsequently rejected
this pass's Greek/Mediterranean world. See
`unwritten-tarot-wild-library-direction.md` for the governing visual direction
and the instruction to produce four previews before expanding again. This v2

October 27, 2026. The reader's direction supersedes the first production pass's
literal suit objects and blanket exclusion of written marginalia:

> Can we have book or books or library related things or bookish things in each
> card? Not overwhelming, just there.
>
> The 10 of Wands could be pens.

## The governing change

The card's action belongs to the Book's world. Its materials, work, places and
small troubles supply the scene. Preserve the traditional card's number,
relationships, gesture and emotional difficulty, while allowing its objects to
change. Keep the existing names, suits, IDs and reading system.

Wands become long wooden dip pens with brass nibs: invention, ambition, messages,
work that gets away from its maker. Swords become paper knives: opening, dividing,
language, a cut that cannot be wished away. Pentacles become round stamped brass
book-cover clasps with a five-pointed mark: material skill, keeping, stewardship,
possession. Cups retain their vessels and water, within reading rooms, borrowed
books, kitchens, tidal libraries and shared tables. Each transformation must be
visually legible, and numbered cards retain exactly their number of suit objects.

Do not rely on placing the same closed book in every corner. A bookbinder at
work, library cart, loose signature, sewn repair, folded map or pressed specimen
can make a scene belong here. Preserve quiet and difficult scenes alike. Books
do not make every picture comforting.

## Marginalia that has something to say

Use one small, related observational study and its short identifying label, plus
one brief reading observation, question or occasional remark from the Book.
Leave breathing room. The main picture still reads first at thumbnail size;
the annotations reward opening the existing full-screen preview.

Name only what has deliberately been drawn. Prefer accurate common names over
invented botanical precision. No random flowers, false species labels, extra
suit objects in the margins or illegible imitation writing.

The Book uses ordinary words, physical attention, occasional mischief and short
sentences. It is a little feral, not babyish. A note points to a possible reading;
it does not declare what is happening in the reader's life.

Lettering is authored data, rendered in the card composition using the existing
handwriting font. The illustration supplies the study, leader marks and quiet
paper; it does not generate exact copy. This preserves spelling, lets the
full-screen viewer enlarge the same lettering and supplies a screen-reader
transcript. Titles and numerals remain in the existing native footer.

## Production and acceptance

Preserve the first pass and rejected variants. Stage revision images, actual
prompts, source receipts and card-specific scene/annotation specifications under
`output/imagegen/unwritten-deck-v2/`. Review the first six together before
continuing. Review exact counts and gestures on every remaining card. Never
crop a missing symbol into compliance or accept a beautiful wrong count.

Install a complete reviewed revision through the stable existing asset names.
Keep the already book-centered, reversibly composed back. Keep the existing
tap-to-preview and contextual interleaf routes. Add literal new scene subjects
to those routes, without making every library mention match the whole deck.
Do not draw a new reading merely because a picture appears between Pages.

Validation covers saved assets, exact authored copy, symbol review and source
checks. It does not claim an app build, installation, native interaction,
performance or print proof. The user has explicitly prohibited builds unless
requested.
