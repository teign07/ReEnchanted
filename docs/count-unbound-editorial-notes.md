# The Count Unbound — editorial and integration decisions

5 September 2026. Companion to the [revised manuscript](count-unbound-creator-pack.md). The [original supplied pack](count-unbound-original-author-pack.txt) is preserved unchanged. This is an authored revision and implementation brief, not runtime implementation or release verification.

## What changed and why

The original has a strong plot and several excellent images. The reciprocal doorway, the honest-but-incomplete headcount, Dracula correcting his own novel, the wrong castle, and Serenity refusing Wicker at the Kitchens door are worth keeping. The main editorial weaknesses are repeated explanations of the moral, similar polished voices across the cast, and too little ordinary acquaintance before the injury. The narrator sometimes knows exactly what every small gesture means and tells the reader immediately. That leaves little to discover.

The revision keeps the causal plot and makes the telling more physical. Wicker's care is a badly held water jug, a map-case clasp he knows how Serenity uses, and a paper fold he waits to close. Permancer has to walk behind his own instrument. Serenity gets lunch, laughter, irritation, an imaginary coastline, and prospective pleasures beyond the investigation. The Book holds an actual cover open and breaks a stitch doing it. These actions can carry the themes without a verdict after every exchange.

Good enough to keep developing: yes. Finished enough to call the prose great: that needs a cold read of the composed branches and actual reader judgment. Mechanical voice checks are useful for defects, not proof of literary quality.

### The voice division

| Speaker | What drives the voice | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| The Book | Physical appetite, curiosity, possession, small grudges, doing something while telling | Omniscient diagnosis of feelings; compulsory lessons; saying “I” on every paragraph merely to signal character |
| Serenity | Quick detours, play, impatience with being solemnly understood; direct refusal when needed | Making her an unbroken stream of wise boundary statements; making joy disappear after October |
| Wicker | Verbal speed, provocation, pride in being hard to surprise; loss of fluency when exposed | An apology aphorism in every scene; cruelty automatically presented as flirtation |
| Penny | Exactness, corrections, curiosity she tries to file properly | Every line being a maxim about evidence; public gossip claiming private romance |
| Ambrose | Concrete food, practical interruption, ordinary competence | A philosopher who happens to carry a ladle |
| Permancer | An adventurer who rehearses his knots; precise speech that becomes plainer under strain | A pure bureaucrat; speeches that explain his fault before he acts differently |
| Soren | Arranged discoveries, patient wit, delight in another person's invention | Perfect gentle rival; an emotional solution delivered as a map |
| Dracula | Controlled formal courtesy, exact desire, evasion exposed by questions | Book-child voice; constant ornate villain speeches; romantic framing of the bite |

The narrator belongs to the Book. Dialogue belongs to each speaker. The prose can use a longer paragraph when a room needs to breathe; short sentences don't require a blank line after every sentence in the final compositor.

One lore-document inconsistency was encountered: `docs/lore-bible.md` calls the Book a “servant” while `docs/the-book-is-a-character.md` explicitly rejects that register. The user's standing voice instruction and the character doctrine guide this revision. This brief does not silently edit either source.

## The month now has four real-world crossings

| October 22–31 | Go back once | Return to an earlier personal observation | Two actual visits face each other in the Book and bound edition |

The weeks govern first offers, not four hard deadlines. Invitation and other-side returns remain reachable until October 27. Week Four remains available through October 31 and has a meaningful personal result even after the crisis ends. No sequence completion bonus. No four-part progress display. No compulsory return to a location. No requirement that a finding show improvement, emotional change, or new information.

One crossing per week is a floor for authored opportunities, not a cap on ordinary noticing. Existing Favors, souvenirs, Compass, capture, and personal Grimoire activity continue to do their jobs. The issue provides reasons to notice and occasions for the kept details to matter.

### A precise eligibility change

The old pack says that only the invitation mission creates participation. That rule is superseded for this revision:

- Any kept reader-authored lived finding from Weeks One–Three, while the event is live and before its climax closes, may establish a lived October contribution.
- The chosen return method plus that contribution plus an encountered ending can yield the method's relic.
- Only an actual invitation finding permits the claim that the reader supplied the return invitation. Another weekly finding never impersonates it.
- Week Four's paired leaf is its own reward and evidence. An after-climax return cannot retroactively help return Dracula or mint that return's relic.
- Story choices and actual Jump participation are retained as fictional conduct in all cases. A reader who plays but writes no field sentence has still played. The internal distinction should be called **lived contribution** where possible, rather than telling that reader they “didn't participate.”

Relic eligibility remains backstage. If a reader keeps a Week One anchor and chooses Hunt, their private history can truthfully say they carried an anchor into the Jump and kept the two-sided bookmark; it must not say their real doorway closed the breach.

### Permission is resolved at each use

Each contribution needs separate authority for (a) using the observation's meaning in fiction, (b) exact quotation, and (c) inclusion in a bound export. Reuse the existing atomic contribution and reading-boundary mechanisms. A private wording choice may allow a nonverbatim reference only when the UI states that clearly. A full do-not-use setting excludes the finding from interpretation as well as quotation. The archive remains readable to its owner.

Never turn a photo into unstated facts. Confirmation of a paper-room design describes the fictional construction, not proof that the real photo contains it. A reader may correct the Book's interpretation; the raw sentence stays unchanged.

Changing quotation permission later must affect future generation and new bindings. Already-exported PDFs are snapshots, not silently editable files. Rebinding resolves current permissions against the source records. Withheld material gets the appropriate authored generic wording, not an empty quotation.

## The braid carries steps when they happen

“When they happen” means when the reader actually encounters or acts on the step, or receives its truthful report. A calendar phase being active does not prove the reader saw the scene. It cannot authorize a braid that says they entered Dracula, comforted Serenity, or chose a return route.

The app already has relevant seams:

- `AuthoredContentReceipt` and `SurfacePage.authoredContentReceipts` retain scoped content, choice, and evidence-page references.
- `UndertakingSerial` distinguishes what happened in the world from what the reader met. Apply the same distinction here through the authored-content ledger; do not add a second serial attendance ledger.
- `SceneWorldBeat` / `SceneWorldCanon` supply the existing braid scene plan; `BraidContributionReceipt` carries world-source provenance on the finished braid.
- `braidToday` narrows the reading interval and records contributing kept pages. It needs eligible non-Page authored receipts alongside that interval, not a reconstruction from whatever event is currently active.
- `MonthlyEditionBuilder` and the PDF writer already bind braids and fiction. They need the actual encountered branch and contributions in chronological context.

These are integration opportunities, not claims that the exact new handoffs are implemented.

### Proposed small extension to authored content

Give each authored beat an optional **braid direction** containing:

1. The content/run/occurrence identity and its canonical event fact.
2. Separate clauses for encountered scene, selected choice, actual lived finding, or received public report.
3. One concrete lasting trace that the beat actually establishes.
4. Forbidden advances or revelations: what must not be known yet.
5. Whether this is a major step that needs a passage, a small echo, or silent background.

Keep this attached to the existing native scene/authoring data. It must not be another model-written canon summary or a parallel quest system. Internally retain exact source identities; in prose the Book remembers the occurrence naturally.

### Selection and completion rules

- On the next user-initiated braid, gather new eligible authored encounter/action receipts since the last committed braid boundary. Also retain relevant earlier contributors to a currently encountered payoff.
- Major steps in that interval receive a concise commissioned passage; don't merely add them to a pool where a generic atmospheric beat can always outrank them. If several occurred, compose one chronological movement with each major fact accounted for, rather than giving every scene a recap paragraph.
- Minor school/romance echoes may appear only where they earn space. They can't crowd out the reader's lived material or reveal unchosen branches.
- An event-only night remains possible as a fictional chapter when the reader actually encountered content. It must not invent a real-life receipt to satisfy a generation precondition. The existing capture-only guard needs an explicit eligibility extension before this can ship.
- Reading nothing new produces no “today you…” event clause. A public report can recount Academy history in third person.
- Selecting a beat isn't the same as publishing it. Only the saved winning braid records which occurrences it covered. Failed generation, cancellation, and unselected alternatives don't consume receipts.
- Rebraiding uses the same contribution window; binding never treats a second version as another event.
- If an authored step happened before midnight but the reader braids after midnight, receipt boundaries retain it. Don't restrict this connection to matching calendar-day IDs.
- A pending minor echo can rest. Major undelivered-to-braid steps may compress into the next permitted passage without a backlog UI or pressure to generate.
- The Book doesn't begin new generation just because a phase changed. This request changes braid content, not the established user-initiated generation policy.

### Illustrative braid passage

This uses a synthetic reader sentence for editorial demonstration, not a real reader record: *The café had turned one chair towards the sun.* It assumes quotation permission, the confirmed “place made” interpretation, an encountered Rule ending, and no unreported physical details.

> You gave me a chair facing the sun. I kept the café where it belonged and took the sentence to the Great Hall.
>
> Tonight the paper threshold left a place open. Dracula saw it. Penny laid down her cards. When Serenity pulled the black thread free, I held the source until its cover bit through a stitch.
>
> He's back in his book. Your chair is still in your sentence. Penny wants me to stop picking at the thread.

The passage doesn't assert the café chair's present physical location. “In your sentence” anchors that last reference to the record. This is the literary job for generation: actual ordinary material, actual fictional turn, and the Book's response in one passage.

## Use the actual Book Jump, with an authored episode policy

The manuscript's supervised Jump should be an authored mode of the existing Jump experience. Merely styling a `.narrativeOS` scene as a Jump would teach the reader a second version of the same object. Conversely, sending October through the existing unrestricted generated expedition would let depth, collapse, costs, and procedural writing conflict with the fixed incident.

### What the current code actually does

`Shared/StoryEngine.swift` defines `BookJumpState`, one optional `ActiveBookJump`, return history, borrowed rules, and resting books. `BookJumpEngine` implements start, advance, stabilize, return, and collapse. A normal start costs 3 Belief; advancing raises depth and degradation; returns with souvenirs can grant a borrowed rule. `dailyDecay` leaves an active Jump where the reader left it.

`CapturePageSheet.bookJumpView` supplies Jump controls. `ContentView.applyBookJumpActionIfNeeded` dispatches only `.bookJump` pages and mutates that existing ledger. `AuthoredStoryScenePageAdapter` currently creates `.narrativeOS` pages. A prewritten scene therefore does not currently create an actual Jump just because its prose says the reader entered one.

`AuthoredStoryScene` also currently carries one opening and a set of choice results. The manuscript's Sunday scene has two decision points and shared continuations. Transcribing the whole thing as one string would leave printed “Choice” instructions in the prose or lose one decision. The native authored sequence needs actual node transitions.

### The recommended implementation slice

Extend the existing Jump state with optional authored-episode identity, current authored node, and existing receipt references. Older saves default to the ordinary expedition. Keep one state machine, one return history, the real ribbon/anchor behavior, normal pause/resume, and shared rendering where appropriate. The monthly authoring manifest supplies the fixed prose and node dependencies. Proposed field names are design suggestions, not names of already-existing APIs.

| Existing action | Supervised episode use |
|---|---|
| Start | Explicitly enter the class Jump; select an allowed existing real anchor or use the fictional class ribbon |
| Advance | Commit a valid authored node/choice and load its fixed continuation; no procedural scene invention |
| Stabilize | May use a true detail to steady the reader's footing where authored; cannot undo Wicker's sentence or prevent the public incident |
| Return | Reader may leave early; the authored emergency return is also a real return transition |
| Souvenir | A fictional Jump recollection is optional, separately attributed; never demanded to escape |
| Borrowed rule | A supervised policy decides whether any rule is granted; don't accidentally grant normal exploration rewards just because return text is nonempty |

The proposed supervised policy waives Belief entry/depth costs, scripted degradation/collapse, and automatic generic return/guide awards for this episode. Permancer supplies the class passage in-world. Ordinary free expeditions keep their existing costs and consequences. No paid content should pretend to offer a consequential control that the authored scene cannot honor.

An explicit early return succeeds. The incident continues among the Academy cast. The reader receives later facts as a truthful report; it cannot be rewritten as though they stayed through the accident. Staying in the Vault makes no Jump-start entry. Simply receiving or opening the story page never starts a Jump.

### Existing active Jump

There is only one active slot today. Never overwrite an ongoing personal expedition to install October. Keep the authored class invitation reachable and offer the report/witness path; let the reader finish their existing Jump through its normal controls if they want the class entry while eligible. If true suspension of one Jump is later desired, design and migrate that capability explicitly. Don't create a hidden second active-Jump store under monthly content.

### Calendar advancement during a pause

The reader's current authored Jump node remains resumable. If October history has advanced while they were away, label the resumed personal scene as the earlier Sunday occurrence. It cannot reopen October, create late lived participation, grant a second relic, or mutate public canon back into a live breach. The reader may instead return directly and receive a current report. “Calendar advanced” never becomes “the reader failed to return.”

### Exact state protection

Use stable run + episode + node + choice identities for idempotent commits. Resume, double tap, sheet dismissal, or generation retry must not charge, advance, record a return, or apply a consequence twice. Validate active episode and expected node before accepting an action. Record actual choices atomically with node advancement. Shared-world occurrence, reader attendance, lived contribution, and reward state remain distinct.

After the aftermath, the manuscript says teaching Jumps are suspended while safeguards are fitted. Resolve that in the app: use a bounded **Academy teaching schedule** closure and reopen with the Protocols fitted, while personal free expeditions continue under their existing policy. If the intent becomes suspending all Book Jump play, that is a larger product decision and must not be silently implemented by a narrative line. The revised Bleed specifically names teaching Jumps.

### Why this is the right seam

The reader learns one thing: entering a Book means keeping a way home. Sometimes they explore freely. Sometimes the Academy leads a particular passage. The same Book remembers both. Fixed canon governs the Academy's incident; real controls govern whether and how the reader personally takes part.

## Romantasy through the school year

User direction: enemies-to-lovers between Serenity and Wicker as the main possibility, another genuine love interest, and reader influence over months. Soren Ng is an editorial proposal for that rival, not a previously established romance. His existing character is grounded in `docs/lore-bible.md`: he arranges discoveries and can hide behind an elegant plan rather than say what he wants.

### What each pairing offers

**Serenity / Wicker:** They like the speed of each other's minds and resent being seen too clearly. She can puncture his performance; he can keep up with her detours. The danger is that he turns everything into a test while she turns away from difficult things. They have to learn whether they can stay interesting to each other when neither is performing. Their early hostility should have unmistakable flashes of pleasure, private attention, and bodily awkwardness; months later, desire must become explicit enough to reward the slow burn.

**Serenity / Soren:** They make a world together. He notices her imagined geography and leaves room for her additions. She draws him into mess, weather, and unplanned company. His weakness produces drama: he can prepare an entire perfect afternoon without asking whether she wants it, and can become hurt when an arrangement isn't understood. She sometimes enjoys being anticipated and sometimes feels hemmed in by it. He has a real claim on the reader's affection, not an assignment to lose gracefully.

**Serenity's own desire:** She wants her map, her jokes, invitations she can answer, room to change her mind, and somebody who can follow a detour without taking over the route. Show what she seeks and does, not only what two people feel about her. Her choices can surprise the reader while still honoring the branch they helped develop.

### October's romantic movement

Before the bite, Wicker sits opposite Serenity without being invited, steals her pencil, and gives her the larger bread half. She draws him into her map. Soren offers a folded route and she lingers over it. The reader has seen a spark with both.

After the attack, Wicker's existing attachment becomes difficult to hide. He botches a practical task, notices her absence, and cannot tolerate his usual joke. His guilt and attraction are different states; the narration doesn't diagnose either as the sole explanation. Serenity's new night sense can later create new experiences they share, but the wound itself isn't why she becomes desirable.

Soren still has a place in her ordinary life. The map scene establishes pleasure during recovery, rather than assigning him a therapeutic role. Wicker's jealousy can be visible in what he doesn't say, with no punishment for Serenity's interest in someone else.

October closes with an opportunity chosen in conversation. No kiss, forgiveness, or couple label yet. The ending gives November a concrete scene to fulfill.

### Draft year trajectory

These are authoring commitments for a proposed serial, not an assumption that existing future issue manuscripts have been changed. Fit them around each month's actual disturbance. Two or three substantial relationship scenes a month are enough when the letters, cast conduct, and braid preserve their consequences.

| November | Fulfill the selected occasion early | A difficult conversation with a real answer; later, one willingly shared laugh | Glass-roof outing; an interruption tests whether he follows her lead | Help arrange an actual encounter; respond when Serenity asks what to make of it |

“Main possibility” gives Wicker prominent authored attention; it doesn't secretly override a reader who has developed Soren's route. Soren gets enough desire, conflict, and specific scenes that choosing him can feel like choosing a life, not declining the intended plot. No romance choice requires a real-life confession from the reader.

### How reader influence becomes real

Use concrete fictional actions: suggest a conversation, choose a companion for a shared task, deliver an explicitly entrusted invitation, advise when asked, or make room for one person to speak. These actions open different authored scenes. Later a plain in-world conversation lets the reader help select the relationship, with Serenity's own stated wishes and mutual interest present in that branch.

Give each route a sequence of meaningful decisions with immediate dialogue and later callbacks. Don't tally generic “niceness” into a hidden romance meter. The existing relationship field can color atmosphere, but cannot by itself establish consent, attraction, exclusivity, or a breakup.

Store decisive state as typed conditions/receipts using `StoryConsequenceLedger`, `StoryConsequenceResolver`, and the authored-content graph. Proposed conditions include a scene having occurred, an invitation accepted, a boundary stated, mutual interest spoken, and a relationship confirmed. Condition names are internal. Add typed states where the current schema can't express them; don't encode durable romance solely in free-form generated prose.

Autonomous cast activity may write small conduct consistent with established state. It must not spontaneously select a partner, erase an explicit refusal, or break up a couple between sessions. Important romantic transitions need authored encounters. Gossip remains rumor unless a character has made the relationship public.

### Shared Academy history and private romantic branches

Dracula's return, Nightbound, and the Protocols remain shared facts. Romantic outcomes are explicitly reader-variable canon inside each private Book. Shared future scenes require conditional references; public reports can't print “Serenity's partner Wicker” for everyone. Later packs must declare which relationship facts they read and provide valid alternatives for Wicker, Soren, undecided, friendship, and neither.

This is a deliberate expansion beyond the original October plan's uniform public history. The private braid, letters, cast dialogue, memory, and physical edition must consume the confirmed branch consistently. If a future authored scene cannot support the alternatives, revise the scene before release rather than quietly resetting a relationship.

## Additional problems caught while composing

1. **Duplicate invitation quotation:** the original common climax and Hunt insert could both quote the sentence, despite an exactly-once rule. The revision gives quotation one common socket and keeps all route inserts quote-free.
2. **Two scene choices in a one-choice payload:** Sunday needs real authored nodes. Visible production headings are not an interaction implementation.
3. **A private sentence's rights:** “Keep inside my own Book” is ambiguous unless it explains whether the observation can still inform fiction. Separate interpretation from quotation and honor do-not-use.
4. **Only one eligible finding:** the original participation rule would ignore three new weekly returns. The revised lived-contribution rule accepts the first three weeks and keeps invitation-specific claims exact.
5. **Route/relic assumptions:** an invitation finding without a chosen route does not imply a route relic. Casebook clauses must check each fact independently; no dangling relic placeholder.
6. **Current state vs encounter:** being delivered a page is not proof of making its choices. Entering the Jump is an explicit action. A report isn't attendance.
7. **The aftermath is a real change:** “Jumps suspended” must reconcile with actual availability; the bounded teaching closure is specified above.
8. **Original pack ended mid-thought:** the supplied file stops at “The last feeling is not victory.” The revision supplies actual endings rather than inventing what its missing final sentence was.
