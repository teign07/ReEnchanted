# Pigment on the living leaf — 12 October 2026

Capture and Place are ordinary folded field notes. Their lettering and drawings are present immediately; they do not inherit the living Book's ink arrival. Existing tabs, seven destinations, Weather availability and accessible dismissal are preserved.

The living leaf uses `BookPigment.metal` through the shared `BookInkMaterialPhase`. Rendered colours supply the pools, fibre marks and displaced strokes. Paper-coordinate noise stays still while pigment gathers. Emergence passes through dilute stains, wicking points, contracting strokes and crisp drying. Submission wets and darkens the original marks, breaks up their cohesion, and leaves a short-lived submerged residue. Shader endpoints are exact original pixels and transparent pixels respectively.

The current source targets a one-second leaf clock. Stains begin as soon as that clock advances, with the initial haze reaching its strength in 0.06 seconds. Individual marks start gathering into strokes within a 0–0.33-second span and bleed for approximately 0.52–0.63 seconds. UIKit sentences and prepared illumination regions gather within a 0.40-second span and each receive a full 0.56-second pigment phase. These proportions match the previous 1.5-second effect, so nearby sentences retain their different, shuffled start bands and later starts still finish later. Submission remains 1.65 seconds. The previous immediate-onset Release passed device review; the one-second revision passed Debug build, installation and fresh-interleaf review on Rabbit.

Regional timing images encode eight start bands in three binary colour channels, so colour-space conversion cannot compress a grayscale delay. Photo treatment, shadows, transparency and multiply blending are excluded from timing-image rendering. The first photo, margin note and ornament in a prepared illumination occupy different bands. The actual artwork keeps its existing colour treatment and opacity.

UILabel draws its actual attributed typography once into a transparent image. A TextKit sentence map supplies overlapping clocks. A temporary SwiftUI host displays the pigment phase; the native label resumes drawing at the deadline. Moving between windows cancels the old host. The shared editor and standalone Capture Text editor take a viewport snapshot after focus and dictation stop; the saved contribution continues to come from the existing frozen input, never the rendering. PencilKit supplies its own drawing image at the visible zoom and scroll position, since its Metal surface cannot be captured by UIKit layer rendering. The original controls, layout and text binding remain in place.

Illustrations and handwritten marginalia use their existing individual arrival seams. Illuminated photo compositions separate paper from pigment. Photos, decorations and marginal text have distinct clocks; pre-rendered library illuminations receive transparent pigment and timing images upstream of page turns. Only a visible arrival asynchronously decodes its pigment image; offscreen curl neighbours retain no decoded pigment texture. Their temporary cache is limited to eighteen compressed entries and 12 MB, with a 1000-pixel longest edge. If that cache is evicted or preparation fails, the finished readable illustration remains available. At rest the existing full-resolution prepared composition is used. Ephemera paper remains present while its contents emerge.

Reduce Motion and VoiceOver bypass the pigment transitions. A debug-only “Pigment tuning leaf” preview combines the production prose label, handwriting, illustration, illuminated sample photo and reader editor, with replay and sinking controls. It does not write a Keep.

Emergence is now reserved for the first leaf of each logical Page and the interleaves. Continuation and response-only leaves show their settled text, marginalia and artwork immediately. The existing folio interaction store remembers each eligible arrival for its reading session, using document identity rather than a paginated leaf identity. Font-size changes and curl-host recreation therefore do not earn another emergence. Leaving an arriving leaf settles its clock, so a quick return also finds dried ink. A new folio session starts with a fresh interaction store. Keep's sinking behavior is unchanged.
