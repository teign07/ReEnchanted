## The Strava calculation

The original passage combined a branded fitness drop-off headline, a claim about most New Year's resolutions, a twelve-day willpower allowance, and the habit study. Those are different quantities; they cannot establish a universal endurance-versus-habit equation.

The trail included Strava's [2019 Year in Sport press book](https://blog.strava.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/YIS-2019-Press-Book_US-1.pdf), the company's [current use of Quitter's Day branding](https://business.strava.com/resources/no-off-season), and its contemporary press statement reproduced by [Sports Insight](https://sports-insight.co.uk/news/strava-reveals-2020-quitters-day/). The full press-book fetch exceeded the browser tool's size limit. A quantitative method supporting the manuscript's population-wide inference was not verified. Even a most-common quitting date would not establish a cumulative majority, or measure willpower biologically.

The invalid arithmetic was removed from the chapter and its notes. The case for ongoing inspiration and a shared circle remains.
