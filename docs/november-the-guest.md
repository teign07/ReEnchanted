# The Guest Who Came on All Souls — November 2026 story

The story for the Kitchen pack ([kitchen-november-pack.md](kitchen-november-pack.md)). That plan
holds the recipes, the Pantry, the jars and the house laws; this one holds what happens at the
Academy. Agreed with bj 2026-10-19.

**Thesis:** food is memory you can make again. **The month's question:** who gets fed, and what
does feeding them bind you to?

October asked who may be invited across a threshold. November asks what you owe someone once
they're inside.

---

## The Hob

**Before.** For centuries the Academy Kitchens kept a hob: a household spirit that scrubbed the
pots at night for a bowl of cream. Nobody thanked it. That was the law. Then somebody did: they
left it a new coat by the hearth. By the oldest rule there is, a thanked hob has to go, and it went.

**The wandering.** Nameless and unfed, it went hungry for a very long time. Hunger is what turns a
brownie into a boggart.

**The Court.** The Unseelie Court found it and fed it. Eat at the faerie table and you belong to it
(Thomas the Rhymer, Persephone's six seeds). It ate everything. It has served the Court since.

**The return.** It comes back on All Souls, when the departed come home. It isn't here on its own
account. The Court has sent it to collect.

**What it collects.** The Unseelie live on what mortals forget. While the Hob eats at the
Academy's table, the memory in the food is carried off to the Court's feast. Soups go flat.
Recipes in *The Middlemost Cookery*, Trencher's estate-sale cookbook, lose a letter at a time.

**Why nobody can stop it.** Guest-right. A guest who has eaten at your hearth three nights can't be
harmed or turned out, and the Court knows that law better than anyone. Trencher's kindness let it
in. Hospitality protects it.

**Two things at once.** At night it's still the Hob: every morning the pots are scrubbed and
stacked, and nobody did it. At the table it's the Court's: it eats slowly, it watches, and
something in the room is gone afterwards. It never says thank you, which is good fae manners. It
never refuses a second helping.

**The Book is afraid of it.** The Book smells through its paper ("paper remembers every kitchen").
At first the Guest smells of nothing at all, *like a kitchen that isn't there any more*. At night,
frost and old iron. The Book keeps its leaves shut after dark and never offers it so much as a
crumb from its gutter.

**Why Trencher won't be thanked.** He inherited the Kitchens' old law without knowing why it was
law: you don't thank the people who keep the kitchen. The Duskthorn etiquette already in the lore
(never a flat "thank you" to the fae, say "I'm grateful") is where that law came from.

## How it ends

The Court's hold is broken the way it was made, with food: a meal given freely, made for the Hob as
itself, with no thanks asked. Trencher has practised exactly that his whole life.

The page Trencher has hunted *The Middlemost Cookery* for, for thirty years, is the Hob's own bowl:
the meal the Academy used to leave out for it. His dream dish is its way home. The Reader's
November cooking restores the page, letter by letter, so he can make it.

**Three endings**, all available through the Reader's last choice. The cooking tally restores
letters and never gates an ending (bj, 2026-10-19). None punishes them.

| Ending | Gate | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | The Reader chooses to send it home | It's the brownie again. The Court's collection fails, and the Court remembers it in February. |
| **The Kitchen's hob** | The Reader asks it to stay | It lives under the Academy's roof and law. The bowl is set out nightly. The Court is still owed. |
| **The Court** | The Reader lets it return to the Court | It goes back with everything it ate. The Cookery keeps its gaps. Trencher keeps cooking. |

In Home and Stay it says **"I'm grateful"** before the door closes, the only correct thing to say
to the fae, and it means it. Then Trencher does what he has never done: he lets someone finish
thanking him.

## Romance

### Serenity, Wicker, Soren

Since October's bite Serenity can't taste anything. In a month about food, that's her private
wound, and when the Academy's food goes flat everyone finally knows what it's like. "Now you know."

The Hob can't take anything from her plate, because there's nothing on it to take. She's the only
one at the table it can't feed from, so she's the first to notice what it's doing. Both of them were
marked by the night, and it's drawn to her.

The triangle plays out through feeding her:

- **Wicker** cooks for the first time in his life, badly, with nerve. He once wrote a sentence
  inside *Dracula* to show off for her. He knows exactly what a bargain costs.
- **Soren** builds her a trail of tiny tastes across the Academy, the long way round, and is the
  first to see the shape of the trap.
- The faerie-tale rule they all know after October: **accepting food from someone binds you.**
  She decides whose food she takes, or cooks for herself.

**October's choice carries forward** (cast-memory recalls on the `count-unbound.november.*` keys).
The one thing she half-tastes is the evening the Reader chose for her: the tea on the glass roof
(Soren), the tart under the cloth that Trencher found in the morning (Wicker), or what she made
for herself (her own evening).

**The Offer.** The Hob offers her taste back, for that evening's memory. **The Reader decides
whether she takes it** (bj, 2026-10-19).

- **Taken:** she can taste again, all of it. She no longer remembers that October evening. Wicker
  or Soren still does, and the Cast's memories keep it for her. That's the dark, romantic heart of
  the month: someone who remembers a night with you that you've given away.
- **Refused:** she stays unable to taste, keeps the night, and the Hob stops watching her quite so
  closely.

The triangle never gets a verdict either way. Serenity chooses; the Book doesn't. No "forgive"
in the margin (October's rule holds).

### Trencher and Boggle *(optional, bj to decide)*

She keeps the rooms; he keeps the table: the Academy's two practitioners of unglamorous magic. She
has tried for years to finish a sentence of thanks. In November she learns the right words, and on
the last day she's the one who cooks for him. A slow, grown-up counterweight to the triangle.

## Thorne, kept ambiguous

Thorne never eats in a room the Hob is in. She knows exactly whose it is. She can't act openly
without revealing what she is (secret canon: Unseelie). **Whether she's protecting the Academy or
the Court sent it with her knowledge is never settled** (bj, 2026-10-19). February's Thorned
Bargain tilts it.

- Momort, whose Duskthorn etiquette slips, warns the Reader: never tell it thank you.
- On the last morning there's a bowl at the Kitchen door with a thorn beside it.
- Behind it all: the Academy owes the Court something old. Tam Lin's tithe (every seven years the
  faerie court pays a teind) is the shape of it. Saved for the year arc; November only shows the
  edge.

## The month, scene by scene (2026 dates)

Eight scenes. Long and immersive, October's register (Gaiman-ish modern faerie tale, the Book's
voice; the Book has no hands). Pinned beats need a floor, not a score.

1. **The Knock** — All Souls' Eve, Sun Nov 1, night. Souling at the west door. A soul cake, a seat
   by the fire. Trencher feeds it first and doesn't ask its name. It asks the Reader's.
   *Choice:* give your name / give a false one / give none.
2. **Three Nights** — Tue Nov 3 to Thu Nov 5, Bonfire Night. Guest-right takes hold. The pots are
   scrubbed overnight and nobody did it. Vellum demands an examination; Trencher refuses under
   guest law. Parkin by the bonfire. The first letter goes from the Cookery. Momort's warning.
3. **Flat** — Martinmas, Wed Nov 11. Everyone's food has gone flat. St Martin halves his cloak.
   Serenity: "Now you know." She notices it can't take anything from her plate.
4. **The Rescue** — mid-month. Penny's attested inventory shows the only recipes keeping their
   letters are the ones someone is making. The Academy starts cooking. Min-seo asks the Guest
   what it needs. The Reader's worked recipes count from here.
   *Choice:* bring a recipe from your own Kitchen to the table / go and rescue one.
5. **The Offer** — about Nov 19, night. Taste for a memory. Wicker and Soren each react,
   according to October's branch.
   *Choice (the Reader decides for Serenity):* take it / refuse it.
6. **Stir-up Sunday** — Sun Nov 22. Everyone stirs the pudding and wishes, the Guest too. Its
   wish gives away what it's looking for: home. Trencher opens the Cookery to the page.
7. **A Place at the Table** — Thu Nov 26, Thanksgiving for readers who keep it, an ordinary
   long-table supper for everyone else. Who gets a place. A chair set for someone absent, echoing
   All Souls. Trencher and Boggle's thread is included.
8. **The Bowl** — Mon Nov 30, St Andrew's Day. Trencher cooks the dish. The Reader chooses:
   send it home / ask it to stay / let it go back to the Court. The tally never gates this choice.
   "I'm grateful." Trencher lets someone finish thanking him. Next morning: the bowl at the door,
   and the thorn.

## Mechanics this rides on

- **The tally:** recipes the Reader actually makes (`kitchen-made:<id>` keeps, and the Reader's
  own Recipe workings) restore letters in the Cookery. Archive-derived, no new ledger.
- **The Cookery's fading:** a visible count the Bleed can print ("The Cookery: 214 letters
  missing, 17 restored from readers' kitchens") and a pack-art leaf with letters gone.
- **Cast memory:** every scene authors `castMemories` and gossip; October's November keys are
  recalled for Serenity's half-taste and the Offer.
- **Engine need (small):** a surrendered memory. If Serenity takes the deal, her own row for that
  October key is marked given away; her asides stop mentioning it; Wicker's or Soren's still do.
- **The Bleed:** pack desk pieces ship with the month: Cast letters about the Guest, classifieds
  ("LOST: the second half of the shepherd's pie. Last seen in the Cookery."), Trencher's chalked
  one-sentence weather menu in the Academy timetable, Penny's "A Guest Remains" coverage.
- **Outcomes:** like Count Unbound's, recorded so December and February can read them.
