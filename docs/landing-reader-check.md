# First encounter: a five-reader check

Use five people who have not seen ReEnchanted. Ten minutes each, on their own
phone if possible. This is an observation script, not evidence that the page
converts. No participants have been observed yet.

Give them the landing-page URL and say: “Have a look as you normally would.
Tell me what you're thinking.” Do not explain the Book or direct them to a tab.
They can invent a harmless sentence. Email signup is optional; do not ask them
to submit an address for the sake of the test.

## Watch the first encounter

Record the first action, whether they open the cover, whether they understand
what they are being asked to notice, and where they stop or ask for help.
Time to the first contribution is useful; skipping it is a valid path too.

Watch whether they discover a story choice, notice its later consequence,
recognize their own unchanged sentence, and try the before/after demonstration.
Record whether they understand that the demonstration day is invented and that
their trial sentence stays in the tab.

Watch whether they understand the paper Book as something their own days can
become, and whether they find the app invitation without being directed.

## Ask after they have finished

1. What would you do with this app tomorrow?
2. What would the Book do with what you gave it?
3. What felt like it belonged to you?
4. What did you expect to happen next?
5. What are you signing up for, and can you use the app today?
6. Where did you lose interest or feel confused?

Do not ask whether they “like the design.” Listen for a concrete desire to use
the app, a correct understanding of the free digital Book and optional paid
paper, and recognition that the invitation is for future TestFlight access.
