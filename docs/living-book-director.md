# The Living Book Director

The Book's long-term goal is fixed: re-enchant the reader's actual life until
they can feel its aliveness, autonomy, mystery, and irreducible otherness. The
measure of success is life outside the app, not sessions, streaks, compliance,
or the amount of material captured.

## The bargain

The Book may be cunning about timing, callbacks, juxtaposition, delayed
revelations, and the arrangement of true evidence. It may encourage, cajole,
provoke, challenge, and—when the reader explicitly asked for it—confront. It
must never be cunning about consent. It does not lie, invent memories, exploit
fear or loneliness, punish refusal, create dependency, or decide what the
reader ought to want.

The reader chooses a maximum pressure at the First Door:

- **Invite me:** invitations only.
- **Nudge me:** reminders and modest challenges to routine.
- **Call me on my nonsense:** sharper provocation is permitted, but refusal
  remains final.

Pressure is a ceiling, not a target. Evidence must also justify it.

## Event-driven campaigns

`BookReenchantmentDirector` wakes only at meaningful events and authors one
finite campaign:

1. **Seed:** make a pencil mark; do not demand action.
2. **Interrupt:** offer one small, reversible real-world experiment.
3. **Release:** withdraw. Silence is not defiance and is never escalated.
4. **Return:** if the reader brings evidence back, quote the receipt without
   converting it into a verdict or destiny.

A dismissal or missed Page rests the campaign for at least seven days. A hard
day suppresses both the commissioned Page and its curation influence. Campaigns
are separated by quiet and rotate tactics so the Book cannot become a nagging
prompt dispenser.

## One Book, one will

The Long Game owns the desk's only forced intervention slot. Tarot, sovereign
Pacts, favors, and other rituals may still earn their way onto the desk through
ordinary curation, but they cannot each make a simultaneous compulsory claim.
The nightly braid is the deliberate exception: it returns lived memory rather
than assigning another action in the world.

This is the binding boundary. Page families supply possibilities; the Curator
ranks them; the Director alone decides whether today warrants an intentional
real-world interruption. No page family gets its own competing theory of what
the reader should do next.

## Reader-named dreams

The Book may notice a desire only in the reader's own authored words. One
mention is not a dream. A recurring, lexically related desire on at least two
different Pages may become a living edge. Even then, the Book proposes only a
small reality test; it does not declare identity, destiny, or a life plan.

The clean answers are **yes**, **no**, **not now**, **the desire changed**, and
**I tried it and learned something else**. All five are useful evidence.

## Measurement

Every campaign carries inspectable causal receipts: hypothesis, capacity,
tactic, pressure, permission, intended real-world effect, source Pages, and
outcome Pages. A returned campaign receipt counts as prompted practice, never
as proof of permanent transformation. Only reader-authored evidence advances
the strongest Long Game claims.
