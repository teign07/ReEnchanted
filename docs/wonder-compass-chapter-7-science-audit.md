# Chapter 7: science audit and applied additions

Applied October 23, 2026, to the bundled Wonder Compass in `Shared/BookReferenceLibrary.json`.

The chapter's argument remains: give the Spark a specific beginning, make that beginning easier, and bring something you want along with you. The 3 D's, Adventure Coat, and Adventure Bag remain intact. Research supports the ingredients; the book's particular recipe is the author's synthesis, rather than a separately tested intervention.

## Corrections that mattered

| Passage | Finding and applied change |
| --- | --- |
| Motivation and Behavioral Activation | Kept action-before-motivation. Changed the claim that motivation itself is a myth and that movement necessarily changes biochemistry and mood. BA involves meaningful or rewarding activity, not simply forcing any movement. The engine and battery metaphors remain. |
| Exercise study | Kept the striking 91% result. Corrected the year, reversed group percentages, intervention format, and outcome. The original full paper was inspected, including its methods and results. |
| Universal 3× multiplier | Removed the promise that every reader's adventure probability triples. The study does not supply that forecast. The data, comparison, specific-planning lesson, and encouraging conclusion remain. |

| Temptation bundling | Kept 51%. Specified initial gym-visit frequency rather than probability of doing the activity at all. Kept the toddler, cookies, lighthouse podcast, and chips. |
| Adventure Coat | Kept the idea and imagery. Newer evidence supports the broad clothing-and-meaning connection, despite problems with some early results. Replaced unsupported motor-cortex/state-memory and hippocampus instructions with learned starting cues. Changed only “RAS” to “brain” in the existing signal line. |
| Summary and discovery text | Matched the corrected claims in the summary. Changed the synopsis's “cannot fail” to “easy to start.” The opening preview remains unchanged. |

The opening “90% of adventures,” boulder/energy units, dopamine shorthand, threshold magic, and other obvious field-guide imagery remain. The notes distinguish imagery from measured quantities. Useful simplifications were not treated as errors merely because they omit technical detail.

## Small additions, with something to do

- **Rehearse the departure once.** A recent synthesis supports if-then plans and rehearsal. The reader says the mailbox plan aloud and gives Saturday morning a job.
- **Borrow an athlete's beginning.** Preparatory routines have evidence in sports. Coat, shoes, bag, door is an explicitly borrowed application, not a claim that a sports trial tested adventure adherence.
- **Give the bag a little messenger.** Distinctive reminder cues can help an existing intention survive until its opportunity. The purple dinosaur is explicitly assigned a job and placed where the reader will encounter it at departure time.

The newer clothing review strengthens the existing coat paragraph. Qualifications about replication, study design, and transfer to the Compass live in the actual Notes & References.

## Sources

All sources supporting the applied changes are recorded in the book's Chapter 7 notes with links and their relevant limits.

- [Dimidjian et al. (2011), Behavioral Activation's origins and current status](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-032210-104535). Corrected the old note's title and journal.
- [Uphoff et al. (2020), Cochrane review of Behavioral Activation](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD013305_behavioural-activation-therapy-depression-adults). Replaced the old note's conflation of statistical power, effect size, and medication comparisons with an identifiable treatment synthesis.
- [Milne, Orbell & Sheeran (2002), original exercise paper](https://bpspsychub.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1348/135910702169420). Full text reviewed, especially printed pp. 167–168 and 173.
- [Gollwitzer (1999), implementation intentions](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.54.7.493), retained foundational reference; [Sheeran, Listrom & Gollwitzer (2025; online 2024), 642-test synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1080/10463283.2024.2334563). The latter's author manuscript was read. Rehearsal is supported; visualization did not independently predict larger effects in that synthesis, so the added instruction rehearses the actual plan.
- [Milkman, Minson & Volpp (2014), temptation bundling](https://www.katymilkman.com/s/holding-the-hunger-games-hostage-at-the-gym-35pa.pdf). Author-hosted published paper read, including the initial attendance estimate and its decline over time.
- [Adam & Galinsky (2012), enclothed cognition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2012.02.008); [Burns et al. (2019), direct replication](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2018.10.001); [Horton, Adam & Galinsky (2025; online 2023), newer evidence review](https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672231182478). Publisher abstracts and the review's university-hosted accepted manuscript were inspected. The mixed replication history does not justify discarding the whole coat idea.
- [Wood & Rünger (2016), habit research](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-122414-033417). Supports repeated actions acquiring contextual starting cues. [Tulving & Thomson (1973), encoding specificity](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0020071) remains as related memory background with its proper scope.
- [Rupprecht, Tran & Gröpel (2024; online 2021), sports routines](https://doi.org/10.1080/1750984X.2021.1944271). Publisher text reviewed. No separate handgrip technique or universal performance multiplier was imported.
- [Rogers & Milkman (2016), reminders through association](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5510470/). Examined the explicit intention-cue link and distinctiveness relative to surrounding stimuli, rather than assuming that any odd object will motivate someone.
- [Shenhav et al. (2017), mental effort](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-neuro-072116-031526), and [Vadillo, Gold & Osman (2016), glucose-model evidence](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797616654911). Relevant review discussion and publisher abstract inspected.

A current-research sweep also found [Svabo et al. (2026), clothing and environmental education](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/publications/messily-mediating-milieu-enclothed-cognition-in-environmental-edu/). Its forest-walk work is a useful adjacent connection, but the practice-based, interpretive design does not establish a clothing intervention's causal effect. It was not added as stronger experimental proof. No new manuscript claim depends on it.
