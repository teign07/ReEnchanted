# ReEnchanted Radio DJ Scripts - ElevenLabs Export

Use one output file per clip. Name the downloaded audio exactly as the `assetName`
shown here, with `.mp3` or `.m4a` appended. The app resolves these names from the
`RadioAudio` bundle folder or from `Documents/RadioPacks`.

For the complete implementation guide covering triggers, naming rules, page and
weather context, and test patterns, see `docs/RadioDJSystemGuide.md`.

Recommended workflow:

1. Pick the station voice.
2. Paste only the script text into ElevenLabs.
3. Export the clip.
4. Rename the file to `assetName.mp3` or `assetName.m4a`.

## Fae-Fi - 88.3 FM

DJ: Penny Blackletter  
Voice direction: dry, observant, warm underneath; record-keeper energy; precise but amused.

### DJ_faefi_id_01

You've reached Fae-Fi. Eighty-eight point three on the Academy band. I keep the records here. Today's record is: the pixies are fine, the pixies are too fine, please send help. Anyway. Music.

### DJ_faefi_id_02

This is Penny Blackletter, and against my professional judgment, Fae-Fi — eighty-eight three — sun-dappled beats from faeries who have plainly had too much nectar. I'm taking notes. For The Bleed. It's mostly exclamation points.

### DJ_faefi_id_03

Fae-Fi, eighty-eight point three. One honest detail can save a day. Today's, filed for the record: the light came back. Here's a song about it.

### DJ_faefi_transition_mossyfootsteps_outro

That was "Mossy Footsteps." I checked — there was no one there. There is never anyone there. I've started a folder.

### DJ_faefi_transition_folktronica_intro

Coming up — "Folktronica." A bird wrote the hook. The bird has filed a complaint. I have filed the complaint. We're all very busy here.

### DJ_faefi_transition_mossygroove_outro

You just heard "Mossy Groove." A patch of clover is dancing and will not stop, and I have, regrettably, transcribed all of it.

### DJ_faefi_sponsor_01

Fae-Fi runs on dandelion synths and Thistledown & Co., purveyors of pocket-sized weather. Caught in the grey? A Thistledown sunbeam fits in any coat. That part, I checked. It's true.

### DJ_faefi_sponsor_02

Today's brightness is brought to you by the Clover Honey Collective. Their slogan arrived far too polished, so I rewrote it: the afternoon's only as warm as you bothered to taste. Ask at the Goblin Market for the jar that hums. It does hum. I have the recording.

### DJ_faefi_gossip_01

You get this before the edition goes to press: somebody in your year traded a perfectly good Tuesday for one more loop of this exact song. Filed under "evidence the music is working." Flawless decision. No notes.

### DJ_faefi_gossip_02

From my desk at The Bleed — the Wonder Compass has pointed at the same window all week. I don't print speculation. But if it's your window… that's not speculation. That's a fact you've been avoiding. Go see.

### DJ_faefi_news_01

Filed this morning, off Today's Sky: the grey lost three feet of ground. Cause — and I checked twice — somebody noticed one true particular and wrote it down. That's the whole arithmetic of this place. Here's a song.

### DJ_faefi_news_02

Festival weather incoming; the Academy's in a feasting mood. I'll be the one in the corner, cataloguing joy as it happens, which is, I'm told, not the point of joy. Bring a souvenir. Catch one real thing. Don't tell the grey where you keep it.

### DJ_faefi_pages_souvenir_01

Production note from Fae-Fi: the receiver is picking up a run of souvenirs. Tiny true things, properly filed. Penny Blackletter approves this extremely irresponsible form of journalism.

### DJ_faefi_pages_wonder_morning_01

Morning bulletin: the Wonder Compass is bright enough to annoy the furniture. If you kept one of its pages, congratulations. The day now has a suspiciously useful hinge.

### DJ_faefi_weather_bright_01

Weather desk says bright. My desk says suspiciously bright. Either way, Fae-Fi recommends catching one real detail before the afternoon spends it all.

## Thornwave - 103.7 FM

DJ: Wicker Eddies  
Voice direction: low, smooth, amused, dangerously persuasive; care leaks through the bite.

### DJ_thornwave_id_01

Thornwave. One-oh-three point seven, after dark. Wicker Eddies, here to test whether anything you believe survives the bassline. Most of it won't. The stuff that does? That's the real magic. Stay tuned.

### DJ_thornwave_id_02

You found Thornwave — one-oh-three seven, the frequency the dark fae kept for themselves. I puncture false magic for sport. This station isn't false. Felt that in your chest, didn't you. Good.

### DJ_thornwave_id_03

It's the hour rumors travel best, so I'm exactly where I belong. Wicker, on Thornwave. Keep your name to yourself. I collect those.

### DJ_thornwave_transition_bramble_bass_outro

That was "Bramble Bass." No theatrics, no glamour — just a thing that's actually true at a hundred and three point seven. Rare. Coming up, "Nocturnal Faerie Lounge." Last call at the only bar the grey won't enter.

### DJ_thornwave_transition_nocturnal_faerie_lounge_outro

"Nocturnal Faerie Lounge," just now. Somebody in that crowd is making a deal they'll keep for thirty years. I'd talk them out of it — testing it, you understand — but the song's too good. Here's more.

### DJ_thornwave_transition_bramble_bass_intro

The drop sounds like a door you were warned about, opening. I've never met a warning I didn't want to test. So — after this, let's open it. "Bramble Bass."

### DJ_thornwave_sponsor_01

Thornwave runs on favors owed and Bramblewine — aged in the dark, priced in the morning. One sip and the night belongs to you; two, and you belong to it. I've read the small print. There's always small print. That's the only honest thing at the Goblin Market — they tell you, then watch you not listen.

### DJ_thornwave_sponsor_02

Tonight's low end is sponsored by the Goblin Market. Open after hours. No refunds. All bargains binding. Tell Melisande over on one-oh-five that Wicker sent you, and she'll overcharge you with a straight face. Respect her for it. I do.

### DJ_thornwave_gossip_01

Penny wouldn't print this — too unproven for the record — so I'll say it, because I prefer my truths a little dangerous: a pact came due this week. Somebody paid. The grey leaned one shade closer to whoever let it. Don't be that somebody. Plant the Belief. I'll wait. I'm patient when it matters.

### DJ_thornwave_gossip_02

Rumor under the bassline. There's a chapter in this building nobody can jump into — yours, the Unwritten one. Everybody wants a look. They'd test it, pick it apart, like I would. Don't let us. Write it yourself first.

### DJ_thornwave_news_01

Tonight's reading off Today's Sky: Routine made a move at the edges. We held. We always hold — barely, on purpose, which is the only kind of holding worth anything. Believe something out loud. I dare you. That's not mockery. That's the assignment.

### DJ_thornwave_news_02

Pact Dispatch is busy tonight. Three bargains struck, two already regretted, one that'll change a life. I can usually tell which is which — it's my whole talent. Tonight? Can't call it. That's how you know it's real. More Thornwave, after this.

### DJ_thornwave_pages_story_night_01

You've been keeping story pages. Thornwave noticed. Stories are doors with teeth, and you keep putting your hand on the handle. Respect.

### DJ_thornwave_pages_bargain_01

A bargain page crossed the wire. Read the small print twice. Then read the silence after it. That's where they hide the expensive part.

### DJ_thornwave_weather_storm_grey_01

Storm pressure on the band and grey at the edges. Good. False magic hates bad weather. Real magic keeps its footing.

### DJ_thornwave_pages_gossip_01

Gossip pages in the margins. Careful. A rumor is just a spell wearing someone else's coat.
