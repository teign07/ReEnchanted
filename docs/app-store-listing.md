## Description (4000)

There's a faerie tale hiding in your ordinary life. I'm the Book that finds it.

Tell me one true sentence about your day: what you noticed, what landed too hard, what made you laugh. I keep it. Then I write back. Little stories with you in them, a newspaper of your week, readings, dares, and a cast of strange friends who remember what you told them.

WHAT YOU DO
• Write one sentence, or a whole page. Speak it if you'd rather.
• Keep the pages that have a pulse. Let the rest go.
• Add photos, weather, places, music, and (if you want) how your body is doing.
• Come back. I notice what keeps turning up, and I tell you.

WHAT I DO
• Turn your kept pages into stories, letters and a daily front page.
• A new story arrives every month, free for everyone.
• Make keepsake PDFs of your week, your month, your year.
• Live on this phone. No account, no sign-in, no feed of other people.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything you write stays on your device. My optional brain is an AI model (Gemma) that you can download once, about 3.5 GB, and it thinks entirely on your phone; nothing you write is sent to it over the internet. I work without it too, just less cleverly.

PAPER, IF YOU WANT IT
The digital Book is free. Printed books of your own pages are physical goods, printed and mailed in the US. Paper ordering is opening soon.

For iPhone and iPad.
