## Evidence boundaries

The seven source notes sit in the existing references for the relevant chapters. They distinguish the studies from the book’s practical applications and retain the main limits: observational associations versus causal claims, structured training versus a single invitation, variation across populations, adherence, and immediate versus lasting outcomes.

The appreciation paper’s published abstract and publisher-supplied study information were accessible, but the complete methods remained inaccessible. Its body passage therefore uses only the verified broad association between appreciation and perceived meaning. It makes no specific experimental or lasting-benefit claim.

The rest passages support recovery and remembering. They do not validate the chapter’s existing brain-wave explanation or assign the wakeful-rest findings to its sixty-second exercise. The existing DMN redemption passage is intact.

## Scope and verification

This integration adds text only. It does not remove or replace existing paragraphs, examples, stories, summaries, or references. Chapter IDs, previews, metadata, and ordering remain unchanged.

Verification compares the manuscript with a snapshot immediately preceding this integration and reconstructs each changed body using the exact insertion manifest. All other bundled entries must match the snapshot, including the earlier Chapter 1–2 and novelty additions. JSON parsing and whitespace checks are used; no build is run.
