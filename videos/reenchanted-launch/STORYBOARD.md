# STORYBOARD.md — ReEnchanted launch trailer

- **Duration:** 60s · **Format:** 1920×1080 landscape
- **Audio:** Music bed = `fae-fi-mossy-groove.m4a` (first ~60s, gentle fade-out at end). No narration — on-screen serif text carries it. Real game SFX punctuate interactions.
- **Message (the ONE thing):** ReEnchanted turns the ordinary day you actually lived into a fairy tale you can keep — a living book with a whole world behind it, free and private.
- **Arc:** Ordinary → the day arrives as pages → keep what's true → it braids into *your* story → a whole living world behind it → it's yours (free/private/open) → come play.
- **Audience:** Curious iPhone users who love books, journaling, cozy/literary games, and a little wonder.
