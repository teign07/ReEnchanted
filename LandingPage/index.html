Who we’re up against

A Curse is eating your days, and it isn’t your fault. I can prove it.

It’s called the , and it doesn’t roar. It just makes things less. Your commute stops having a smell. Your street turns into “my street.” The kettle you loved becomes an appliance and sits there being one.

You recognised at least one of those. That’s the proof. Give it a year and your whole life reads as a summary of itself. Accurate. Missing everything.

I’ve fought it for centuries. The fight takes lifetimes. It might never end. Fine. You don’t cure it: you drive it back, one exact detail at a time, and the details have to be real ones, out of your actual Tuesday.
