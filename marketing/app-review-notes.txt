ReEnchanted is a free, local-first journal that answers back as a living book. Everything the reader writes stays on the device. There is no account, no sign-in, no In-App Purchase, and nothing shared between readers.

Printed books. Readers can order printed books of their own pages. These are physical goods printed and mailed by Lulu and paid with Stripe (Guideline 3.1.3(e)); nothing digital is sold. In this build paper ordering is closed: the Bookshop (Contents → The Bookshop) says "Paper opens soon", and no payment can be started. Making a PDF of your own pages is free and works.

The on-device model. An optional ~3.5 GB download (Gemma, from Hugging Face) is offered during onboarding. It is model weights, not executable code, and it runs entirely on the device. The app is fully usable without it; you can tap "Not yet" and keep writing.

Let's Chat is a conversation with that on-device model. Nothing typed there leaves the device. Every conversation says the replies come from an AI model and can be wrong; holding a reply offers "Erase this reply" and "Report it" (a pre-filled email to hello@reenchanted.app that the reader chooses to send).

Permissions are each requested from a visible action, with the reason said first: HealthKit (optional Body Pages, read only, no diagnosis or advice), Location (where a kept page happened, and its weather; the Book asks in its own words before iOS does), Photos and Camera (pictures on pages), Microphone and Speech (dictation), Calendar (the Book asks before iOS does) and Reminders (optional), Notifications (off by default), Face ID (optional lock). Everything works with every permission denied.

Health data and print. Health-derived pages are excluded from printed books by default. Only if the reader turns on "Include Fuel & Inner Weather" for their own printed volume, and then orders it, does a summary of those pages appear in the PDF sent to the printer, to be mailed to that reader. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or anything else.

Public-web lookups (off by default) use Wikipedia's search API, the GDELT news index, and public research APIs, and return text the app writes into the reader's pages. There is no in-app browser.
